## 🏗️ Project Overview

Construction projects frequently leave behind usable materials like cement bags, steel rebar, bricks, tiles, and pipes. Sellers often struggle to find immediate buyers and end up paying hauling fees, while other local builders and homeowners need affordable materials nearby.

**ReBuildX** bridges this gap through a specialised construction-focused exchange:
- **Hyperlocal Radius Matching**: 10–20 km radius search for fast, affordable, site-to-site pickup.
- **Construction-Specific Grading**: Exact material specifications (e.g. 53-grade OPC cement, dry-sealed bags, Fe550 steel).
- **Transport & Delivery Coordination**: Connects users directly with local mini-truck and tempo drivers.
- **Fair Price Guidance**: Benchmark price recommendations to save buyers up to 40% below wholesale retail.
- **Zero Dump Waste**: Diverts usable materials from landfills and cuts debris disposal fees.

## 🌐 Deploying to GitHub Pages

1. Go to your repository on GitHub: `https://github.com/Thanishka1410/ReBuildX-LandingPage`
2. Navigate to **Settings** > **Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment** > **Branch**, select `main` (or `master`) and `/ (root)`.
4. Click **Save**. Your site will be live at:
